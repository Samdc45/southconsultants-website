Course Overview

Falls from height remain one of the leading causes of serious harm on civil construction sites. This foundation course gives site workers and supervisors the knowledge to identify fall hazards, select and use the right fall-protection equipment, and know what to do if a fall occurs.

The course is delivered as four interactive online modules plus a final assessment, hosted on South Consultants' Moodle LMS. Each module combines short lessons with a knowledge-check quiz; a certificate is issued automatically on successful completion of the final assessment.

What's Covered

  • Module 1 — Fall hazards on civil construction sites
  • Module 2 — Harness selection, fitting and inspection
  • Module 3 — Edge protection and exclusion zones
  • Module 4 — Rescue planning and emergency response
  • Final Assessment — Certificate issued on pass

Why This Matters in New Zealand

Under the Health and Safety at Work Act 2015 (HSWA), employers must ensure workers have the training, skills, and experience to work safely at height. WorkSafe NZ treats falls from height as a priority enforcement area, with penalties reaching into the millions for serious breaches. Documented, certified training is your primary evidence of due diligence.

Key New Zealand compliance requirement:
Employers must provide site-specific fall protection training before workers are exposed to height risk. This course provides a portable, verifiable record of foundation-level competency that can be shared with site managers and H&S officers on demand.

Assessment & Certification

Each module includes a short knowledge-check quiz. The course concludes with a final assessment; participants who pass receive a South Consultants Certificate of Completion in Working at Heights Foundation, issued instantly through the LMS.

Prerequisites

None. Suitable for new starters and experienced workers needing a documented refresher before working at height.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a substitute for a formal height-safety ticket?

This is a foundation awareness course. Where a site or regulator requires a specific accredited height-safety qualification, check that requirement separately — this course is designed to complement, not replace, formal certification where one is mandated.

How long does it take?

Most participants complete all four modules and the final assessment in under half a day, self-paced.

Yes — the certificate is issued automatically through the LMS as soon as you pass the final assessment.
